Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

Obscuro Mediaworks ("we", "us", "the studio") is an independent game studio based in Buenos Aires, Argentina. This policy covers:

  • MOBA Warmup Gym (the game) and its online leaderboard.
  • Our official social media accounts (TikTok, X, YouTube, Discord) and the tools we use to publish content to them.

1. What we collect in MOBA Warmup Gym

The game has an optional online leaderboard. If you use it, we store:

  • A randomly generated player ID, created and stored on your own device. It is not linked to your name, email, IP address, or any account — we cannot identify who you are from it.
  • A display name you type in ("summoner name"), up to 24 characters. This is free text you choose — we do not verify it against any real League of Legends account. It defaults to "Anonymous" if left blank.
  • Your gameplay results for the leaderboard (score, streak, session count, timestamps), linked only to the random player ID above.

We do not collect your real name, email address, payment information, or precise location. We do not use third-party advertising or analytics trackers in the game.

The leaderboard is hosted on Supabase (our database provider); their infrastructure may log standard technical web request data (like IP address) as part of normal hosting, the same as any web service — this is not something we access or use ourselves.

Deleting your data: since leaderboard entries are anonymous by design, we cannot look you up by name. If you know your player ID (or the display name + approximate date you played) and want your entry removed, contact us at the email below.

2. Our use of social media platform APIs (TikTok, X, YouTube, Discord)

Obscuro Mediaworks uses the official APIs of these platforms only to publish our own content (gameplay clips, updates, announcements) to our own official accounts (@mobawarmup and equivalents). Specifically:

  • We do not collect, access, or store data belonging to other users of these platforms.
  • We do not use "Login with [platform]" to sign in outside users.
  • The only credentials involved are our own account's authorization tokens, used solely to post content we created ourselves.
  • We do not run ads or paid promotion through this tooling — publishing is manual, reviewed by a person before anything goes out.

3. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the game or our social accounts after a change means you accept the update.

4. Contact

Questions about this policy, or a data deletion request: obscuromediaworks@gmail.com
